iDubbbz

* 1. Oktober 1990 (36 Jahre)

Ian Kane Jomha, known online as iDubbbz, is an American YouTube personality, boxing promoter and boxer. The creator of the YouTube channels iDubbbzTV, iDubbbzTV2, and iDubbbzgames, he is best known for his comedy video series, including Content Cop, Bad Unboxing and Kickstarter Crap, his collaborations with numerous other creators and recent foray into boxing and documentary filmmaking.
