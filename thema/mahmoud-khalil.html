Mahmoud Khalil

* 1995 (30 oder 31 Jahre)

Mahmoud Khalil is an Algerian-Palestinian activist known for his role as a negotiator and spokesperson in the 2024 Gaza Solidarity Encampment and broader protests in solidarity with Palestine at Columbia University during the Gaza war and genocide while he was a graduate student at Columbia's School of International and Public Affairs, and for his detention by US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) from March 8 to June 20, 2025, under the second presidency of Donald Trump. Khalil was detained without being accused of a crime.
